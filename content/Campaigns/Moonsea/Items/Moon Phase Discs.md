# Moon Phase Discs

Polished stone discs made to fit nine sockets around the fountain in the [[Moon Plaza]]. One full-moon disc was already fitted when Pug's escort arrived. [[Herschel Blackroot]] found a black new-moon disc among the rubble and fitted it opposite the full moon, causing the hovering [[Moonstone]] to pulse.

Herschel's fey hawk identified two further likely disc locations before the [[Moon Deva]] destroyed it.

During the continuing fight, the escort recovered and fitted the first-quarter, third-quarter, waning-crescent, waning-gibbous, waxing-gibbous and waxing-crescent discs. The final disc completed the mechanism and deactivated the [[Moonstone]].

Session 6 recorded nine sockets around the fountain. In Session 7 the GM counted eight including the full moon, and the mechanism completed once eight discs were fitted. Treat the socket count as eight unless later sessions say otherwise.

**Status:** All eight lunar phases fitted. Removing one disc to reactivate the ward is a proposed next action, not yet completed.
