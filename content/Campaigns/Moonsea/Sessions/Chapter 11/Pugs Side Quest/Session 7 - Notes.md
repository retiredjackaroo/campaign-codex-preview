_Chapter 11, Side Quest, Session 7_

The reunited escort brought down the Moon Deva's Wall of Force, then recovered and fitted the remaining lunar discs under constant fire from the Moonstone. They learned that the deva is a manifestation of Myth Drannor's damaged Mythal and that the city itself must judge them through further trials. Once the last disc was fitted, the Moonstone went dormant. The weakened deva warned them away from the temple, and the party chose to respect it rather than destroy it. See [[Session 7 - The City Must Judge|the narrative]].

## Present

- Active: [[Pug DAstibar|Pug D'Astibar]], [[Herschel Blackroot]], [[Morwyn Sabbath]], [[Ocreata]], [[Ripper]] and [[Mar]]. This is the first session in which all six were played together.
- Summoned: [[Nick Timoney]], destroyed early by the Moonstone's blast, and Pug's spectral companion.
- Opposition: the [[Moon Deva]] and the [[Moonstone]].

## Breaking the Wall of Force

- Morwyn's Dissonant Whispers dealt 7 psychic damage, but the deva held its concentration.
- Herschel's Dispel Magic failed against the wall. When he appealed to the deva as an ally, it replied, “The city has not judged you.”
- Mar arrived carrying Ripper and took her Starry Form (Archer). Her arrow missed, but her starry wisps hit for radiant and lightning damage.
- Pug channelled a 2nd-level Magic Missile through his spectral companion. The four darts forced four concentration saves, and the deva's Wall of Force fell.

## The Deva and the Moonstone

- The deva mostly obstructed rather than killed. It used force shoves, a 20-foot line of rushing water, ribbons of liquid moonlight (restraining Morwyn, then Mar), Maximilian's Earthen Grasp, a gravity sinkhole and Gust of Wind.
- The Moonstone did most of the damage. It spun and then fired 20-foot bursts of radiant lightning, once arcing a second blast, and a force pulse that threw Morwyn 20 feet.
- The deva's words to the party:
  - To Pug: “You have not been chosen as worthy.”
  - To Herschel: “I only harm the unworthy. You have not been judged. The city will judge you.”
  - To Ocreata: “Why do you waste your energy on this? You have not been judged.” The temple is barred to them. The crystal is a defensive ward protecting the temple, and the deva is the barrier to entry.
  - To Mar: the party was trying to disable one of the last defences of the city's most sacred place. If they left, the deva would neither help nor hinder them.
- Ripper used Wrath of the Wild. Her dagger and short sword hit, but the deva was unfazed.
- Herschel took the form of a riding horse and struck the deva with his hooves and a thunderous Primal Strike, but it held its concentration.
- Ocreata hit with a javelin (Slow mastery), and later landed a charge attack plus a critical glaive strike for about 21 damage. He then stayed close to the deva to draw its attention.

## Mishaps and Wild Magic

- Pug's Absorb Elements was twisted by the wild magic. He took full lightning damage, and the ward settled on the empty socket he had been pointing at.
- Morwyn Misty Stepped out of the moonlight ribbons, leaving them behind.
- His first Dimension Door failed (natural 1) and produced only a shimmer. His second produced a sonic boom at both ends and 2 thunder damage.
- The deva's gravity sinkhole dragged Pug 10 feet down and pinned him. He was freed only when the deva switched its concentration to Gust of Wind.
- Ocreata freed Mar from the ribbons with a strong Athletics check.

## Healing

- Mar cast a 2nd-level Healing Word on Pug (16 HP).
- Morwyn cast a 3rd-level Mass Healing Word on all six companions (10 HP each), drank a healing potion and later cast Cure Wounds on himself.
- Ocreata used Second Wind twice.

## What the Party Learned

- **The city judges.** To be judged worthy, the city must find them a friend or ally who will honour its memory and history.
- **Trials in threes.** Ocreata believes Kjeorn's sacrifice to the Binder was one trial. The city “loves to do things in threes”, so more trials await in other places before it decides whether they are worthy, and whether Pug in particular is worthy to inherit its memory.
- **The Mythal.** The deva is a manifestation of Myth Drannor's Mythal. The Mythal is damaged but still functioning. That is why the ruins are a wild magic zone and why Pug's visions are only fleeting glimpses, and it still holds the living memory of the city.
- **The third Coronal.** Pug's visions show the coronation of the third and final Coronal, at the height of the golden age. In that reign, imprisoned fiends returned, gates opened to Avernus and the Abyss, and demons and devils overran the city.
- **The Noble Quarter.** In Myth Drannor, nobility meant service. The Noble Quarter held the magistrates, guilds, archives and civil service, and it is the party's likeliest lead.

## End State

- All eight lunar discs are fitted and the Moonstone is dormant.
- The Moon Deva is weakened and is now the temple's only guardian. It warned that it will destroy anyone who tries to enter the temple, and urged the party, if they seek to be proven worthy, to “go, and be quick about it.”
- Morwyn floated killing the weakened deva. Ocreata, Herschel and Pug rejected the idea, and the party chose to respect it.
- The party plans to remove one disc so the Moonstone reactivates and guards the temple in their absence, and to carry the disc with them. Ocreata volunteered to take the risk. This was deferred to next session.
- Approximate hit points at close: Pug 24/58, Morwyn about 25/51, Ocreata about 67/85.
