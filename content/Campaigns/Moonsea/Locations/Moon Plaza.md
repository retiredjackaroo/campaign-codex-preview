# Moon Plaza

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/The Fountain.png]]

A circular plaza before the [[Temple of Sehanine Moonbow]] in [[Myth Drannor]]. Broken marble, fallen statuary and fragments of coloured crystal cover its pale stone. The plaza is unusually free of battle damage; time, rather than violence, appears to have ruined it.

At its centre stands a fountain beneath a hovering, person-sized [[Moonstone]]. Nine fitted sockets circle the fountain: one initially held a full-moon disc and eight were empty. [[Herschel Blackroot]] recovered a new-moon disc from the rubble and fitted it opposite the full moon, causing the Moonstone to pulse.

When [[Pug DAstibar|Pug D'Astibar]] entered, the plaza showed him a memory of the third Coronal's investiture. Thousands of elves gathered while the new Coronal knelt before the people and declared that honour was service rather than dominion.

Pug's reunited escort recovered the remaining [[Moon Phase Discs|lunar discs]] while the Moonstone and [[Moon Deva]] attacked. When Ripper fitted the last disc, the mechanism glowed and the Moonstone descended into the fountain and became inactive. The weakened deva now stands as the temple's only active defence.

**First appears:** [[Session 6 - Beneath the Same Moon]]
