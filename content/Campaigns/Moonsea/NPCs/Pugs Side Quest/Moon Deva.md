# Moon Deva

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/Moon Deva.webp]]

A vast celestial guardian of the [[Moon Plaza]], appearing cross-legged amid blue and violet light. [[Ocreata]] knew that the plaza had a radiant guardian but had previously fled rather than face it.

The deva manifested after [[Herschel Blackroot]] placed the new-moon disc into the fountain's lunar mechanism. It silently wagged a finger at him, then attacked with Bigby's Hand, bindings of liquid moonlight, a supernatural shove and Wall of Force. It effortlessly deflected Ocreata's glaive but bled when repeated grazing strikes nicked its fingers.

When [[Morwyn Sabbath]] struck a note and commanded it to grovel, the deva answered “No” in precisely the same key.

The guardian is a manifestation of Myth Drannor's damaged but still-living Mythal. It is not the judge of worthiness: the city itself makes that decision through the memories held in the Mythal. Until the party is judged, the deva is bound to prevent them from disabling the city's defences or entering its most sacred temple.

Pug broke its Wall of Force with repeated Magic Missiles, after which the deva used moonlight bindings, rushing tides, gusts, an earthen grasp and a Gravity Sinkhole to keep the escort away from the fountain. Once all eight lunar discs were fitted, it warned that the dormant Moonstone had left it as the temple's sole and weakened defence.

**Status:** Active but weakened; the last guardian barring entry to the [[Temple of Sehanine Moonbow]] until the city judges the party worthy
**First appears:** [[Session 6 - Beneath the Same Moon]]
