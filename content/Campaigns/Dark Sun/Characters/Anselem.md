# Anselem

![[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Assets/Pasted image 20260630164615.png]]

A Fire Urokite whose bond to elemental flame carries both power and political weight on Athas, where defiling magic has scorched the land and elemental forces are venerated as the primary alternative to the sorcerer-kings.

**Status:** Alive  
**First appears:** <!-- link to Episode 1 once built -->

## Relationships

- [[Babs|Her]]: kept her and the rest of the front line standing with repeated Healing Word during the Act II, Session 4 elemental fight

## Notable moments

- **Act II, Session 4:** Fought almost entirely with his fire rather than his glaive, which he has never swung. Tried Command (HALT) on the sand elemental via Magic Initiate, but it made the save that cast. His first Guiding Bolt missed and the elemental turned it on [[Babs|Her]]; his second connected for radiant damage. Spent much of the fight as the party's second healer, landing Healing Word again and again (including a 2nd-level cast on [[Babs|Her]]) to hold the line.
- **Act II, Session 6:** Dazed on more than one turn by the [[Umber Hulk]]'s confusing gaze, losing whole actions to it. Cast Burning Hands blind through [[Pterrence]]'s Fog Cloud on a percentile-chance guess at where the enemy stood, and connected. Later landed a Guiding Bolt on one of the two [[Neogi]] that detonated it outright, spattering the party in remains. Briefly heard the Neogi hive-mind's telepathic chorus but made his wisdom save and resisted its charm, unlike [[Babs|Her]] and [[Koresh Ironblood]].
- **Act II, Session 7:** Burned a fleeing Neogi taskmaster through cover with Radiance of the Dawn and helped diagnose the preservative used on the pantry's dead. Ended the holding-pen battle enthralled by the orange Neogi with two levels of hive strain, after being ordered to attack Koresh with Toll the Dead.
- **Act II, Session 9:** Confirmed his advancement to level 5 and opened with Fireball against [[The Voice]], destroying the cocoon before its occupant could speak. Reached three levels of hive strain and was compelled toward [[The Hive Mother]], forcing the party to restrain him repeatedly. Once the compulsion ended, he placed a second Fireball across Mother, an attendant [[Neogi]] and a [[Hook Horror]].
