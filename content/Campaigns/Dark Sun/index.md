## Where We Are Now

Deep beneath [[The Hidden Spring]], the party has reached the heart of [[The Colony]]. A vast nursery still holds countless Neogi eggs and about a dozen adult guards. In a northern chasm chamber, the party destroyed a cocooned Templar called [[The Voice]], whose final mass suggestion drew [[Anselem]] and [[Kit-Chac]] toward Mother.

![[The Colony - Roll20.png]]
_The resin-lined depths of the Neogi colony._

Kit-Chac reached [[The Hive Mother]] alone and survived until the others arrived. [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk Gulbadani]] has now pulled him back to the group, opening a chance to retreat, but Mother, two [[Neogi]] and a wounded [[Hook Horror]] remain active. Anselem and Tchk'Tchk retain three levels of hive strain; [[Salt]] has two; [[Babs]] and [[Pterrence]] have one. [[Derfel]] and Kit-Chac are clear.

_The Hive Mother remains alive at the end of Session 9._
